Choosing a finish

Think in surface families before choosing the exact tile.

You do not need to know the exact tile name at the start. Begin with a surface family: marble effect, onyx, travertine, limestone, concrete, terrazzo or textured decorative tiles.

From there, Artiling Studio can advise on porcelain suitability, finish, tile size, mitred edges, setting-out and installation details for the room or feature.
