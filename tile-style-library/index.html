How to use this guide

Browse by surface look, colour family and finish.

Not sure what style you want? This guide helps you understand different tile and porcelain looks, from Calacatta marble effect and warm travertine to dramatic onyx, terrazzo and textured handmade finishes.

Use this guide as a starting point for choosing the surface language of a bathroom, sink or feature wall. When a style feels right, mention it in your quote request or send us an inspiration image.

Choosing a finish

Think in surface families before choosing the exact tile.

You do not need to know the exact tile name at the start. Begin with a surface family: marble effect, onyx, travertine, limestone, concrete, terrazzo or textured decorative tiles.

From there, Artiling Studio can advise on porcelain suitability, finish, tile size, mitred edges, setting-out and installation details for the room or feature.
