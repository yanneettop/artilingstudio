Before we begin

Useful project questions.

Have drawings, dimensions or reference images ready? That is enough for an initial conversation.

Can one project combine fabrication and tiling?

Yes. Combined projects let us coordinate the sink, vanity, wall layout and surrounding surfaces as one package.

Do you work throughout London?

We undertake residential and design-led projects across London. Share the postcode when enquiring so we can confirm coverage.

Can you work from drawings?

Yes. Plans, elevations, dimensions, photographs and material references all help us assess scope and fabrication requirements.

When should we get in touch?

Ideally before final plumbing positions and surface orders are fixed, especially for bespoke basins and aligned slab layouts.
