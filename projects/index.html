Project enquiries

Do you create bespoke porcelain sinks in London?

Yes. We make porcelain sinks to measure for bathrooms, cloakrooms and interiors across London.

Can you install large-format porcelain tiles?

Yes. We install large-format porcelain with careful setting out, alignment and edge control.

Can you work with porcelain supplied by the client?

Often, yes. Send the porcelain, drawings and dimensions and we’ll confirm suitability.

Do you handle wet room and bathroom tiling?

Yes. We cover wet rooms, bathrooms and vanity areas where waterproofing and finish need coordinating.

How can I request a quote?

Use the quote form with your dimensions, photos and drawings. We’ll reply with next steps.
