Project overview

One porcelain finish across the sink and storage

The basin, worktop and drawer fronts use the same beige porcelain with a marble effect. Mitred joints connect the panels around the sink and cabinet.

The two drawers open by touch, with no handles fitted to the porcelain fronts. Bronze wall taps sit behind the basin, with the blue wall and its onyx pattern to the left.

Construction details

Three parts of the build

01

Mitred basin

Porcelain panels form the sides and base of the integrated basin, joined at mitred edges.

02

Push to open drawers

Two push to open drawers sit below the sink. Their fronts are faced in the same porcelain.

03

Wall finishes

Bronze taps are mounted on the pale porcelain wall. The blue surface with an onyx pattern sits to the left.
