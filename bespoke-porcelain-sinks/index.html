Best Uses

Mitred Porcelain Sinks

A mitred porcelain sink is made from porcelain slab pieces cut and joined at clean angles, so the basin, front apron and side returns look like one folded stone surface.

This is useful when a standard basin feels too small, too visible or too separate from the room. The sink can become a floating trough, a long linear basin or a made-to-measure wall-mounted piece.

The detail matters at the corners and drainage line: a good mitred edge keeps the porcelain calm, sharp and intentional.

Vanity Composition

Porcelain Vanity Tops

A porcelain vanity top is the surface around the basin: the horizontal top, splashback, side returns and, in this style of work, often the sink itself.

Instead of choosing a basin, countertop and wall finish separately, the vanity area can be designed as one fabricated porcelain composition with brassware, mirror positions and wall surfaces considered from the start.

This gives the room a more architectural look and keeps the practical details, like falls, cleaning and daily use, part of the design.
