Made to measure

Planned around width, depth, wall layout, tap position, drainage, storage and the way the porcelain meets surrounding tiles or joinery.

Why bespoke

A porcelain sink should feel designed into the room, not added at the end.

A bespoke porcelain sink gives the bathroom a cleaner, more architectural finish than a standard basin. Instead of choosing a ready-made size and working around it, the sink can be made to suit the vanity, wall layout, tap position and overall design.

At Artiling Studio, we create made-to-measure porcelain sinks for bathrooms, wet rooms and vanity areas across London, with careful attention to proportion, mitred edges and practical installation details.

For wider fabrication work, see our porcelain fabrication London service for mitred sinks, vanity tops and made-to-measure bathroom surfaces.

02 - Visual proof

MITRED DETAIL

Clean folded edges, made to look built into the room.

Mitred porcelain edges allow the sink to feel more architectural than a standard basin. The surface wraps cleanly around the form, creating a calm, seamless finish.

As with any porcelain bathroom surface, the long-term result depends on suitable material, support, adhesive choice and installation method; The Tile Association Tiling Guide is a useful reference for the preparation principles behind reliable tile and porcelain work.

Ideal for wall-hung sinks, vanity tops and minimalist bathrooms.

VANITY COMPOSITION

Sink, vanity top and splashback can work as one surface.

Porcelain can be used across the sink, vanity top and splashback so the bathroom feels considered rather than pieced together. This is especially useful for compact spaces, feature walls and made-to-measure vanity areas.

Frequently asked questions

Bespoke porcelain sink questions

How much does a bespoke porcelain sink cost in London?

The cost depends on the size, porcelain slab, edge detail, vanity layout and installation requirements. Send us the approximate measurements and a few photos, and we can guide you with a clear quotation.

Can you make a porcelain sink to any size?

Most designs can be made to measure, depending on the space, slab size, drainage position and installation access. We confirm these details before fabrication.

Are mitred porcelain sinks durable?

Yes, when fabricated and installed correctly. Porcelain is dense, water-resistant and suitable for bathroom use, while mitred edges create a clean architectural finish.

Do you also make porcelain vanity tops and splashbacks?

Yes. We can create porcelain vanity tops, splashbacks, shelves and matching bathroom surfaces alongside the sink.
