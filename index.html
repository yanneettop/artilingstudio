Bespoke Porcelain Sink Design

Not a basin added at the end. A surface designed from the beginning.

A bespoke porcelain sink changes how a bathroom feels. The basin, splashback, edge thickness, tap position and surrounding surfaces can be planned as one calm composition, rather than assembled from separate products.

Mitred edges

Clean folded porcelain corners for a refined architectural finish.

Integrated storage

Push-to-open and soft-close drawers can be planned beneath the porcelain surface for a cleaner, more practical vanity area.

Surface continuity

Porcelain, veining and surrounding tiles planned to feel connected.
