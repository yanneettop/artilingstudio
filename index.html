01 Sinks & Vanity Units

Signature Service

Porcelain sinks & vanity units, drawn to the millimetre.

We measure the space first, then design the basin and the surrounding elements to suit the room dimensions, plumbing and intended use. The result is a bespoke porcelain sink and vanity unit drawn specifically for that bathroom.

  • 01

    Vanity units made for the room

    Drawn to the exact width of the opening, with drawer depths based on the bathroom’s storage needs.

  • 02

    Integrated porcelain sinks

    Basin and surrounding surface fabricated from large-format porcelain with mitred joints, for cleaner lines and fewer visible interruptions.

  • 03

    Refined finishes

    Selected against the brassware and tiling already specified for the room.

  • 04

    Precise installation

    We measure, fabricate and install the work ourselves, so joint lines and dimensions are coordinated from drawing through to installation.

04 How it works

How it works

From enquiry to installation.

  1. 01

    Initial Enquiry

    Send us the room, approximate dimensions and the timescale you are working to.

  2. 02

    Design Direction

    We propose a material and a layout, and set out what is achievable in porcelain at that scale.

  3. 03

    Specification & Planning

    Dimensions, finishes and key installation details are agreed before fabrication begins.

  4. 04

    Making & Installation

    The piece is fabricated in the workshop and installed by the same team.

Why Artiling Studio

Sinks, surfaces and installation details planned together.

We plan the basin, splashback, edge thickness, tap position, storage and veining from the same drawings. This keeps mitres, joints and surface lines consistent.

  1. 01

    Samples and drawings reviewed together

    Based in London, we work with homeowners, interior designers and hospitality clients. Samples and drawings are reviewed together, with finishes checked in the room’s own light and fitting details settled before materials are ordered.

  2. 02

    Joints planned for the material

    We resolve mitres, tone matching and surface transitions at drawing stage.

  3. 03

    One coordinated team

    The sink and surrounding surface work are checked against the same drawings, keeping dimensions and joint lines consistent across the project.

  4. 04

    Built for daily use

    Porcelain handles daily wear. We specify the right substrates, fixings and installation method for the space.
