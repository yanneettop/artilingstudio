01 — Sinks & Vanity Units

Signature Service

Porcelain sinks & vanity units, drawn to the millimetre.

We measure the space first, then design the basin and the surrounding elements around the room dimensions, plumbing and intended use. The result is a bespoke porcelain sink and vanity unit drawn specifically for that bathroom.

  • 01

    Made-to-measure vanity units

    Drawn to the exact width of the opening, with drawer depths set around what the bathroom needs to store.

  • 02

    Integrated porcelain sinks

    Basin and surrounding surface fabricated from large-format porcelain with mitred joints, for cleaner lines and fewer visible interruptions.

  • 03

    Refined finishes

    Selected against the brassware and tiling already specified for the room.

  • 04

    Precise installation

    We measure, fabricate and install the work ourselves, so joint lines and dimensions are coordinated from drawing through to installation.

04 — How it works

How it works

Four steps.

Four steps from first enquiry to completed installation, so you know what happens at each stage.

  1. 01

    Initial Enquiry

    Send us the room, approximate dimensions and the timescale you are working to.

  2. 02

    Design Direction

    We propose a material and a layout, and set out what is achievable in porcelain at that scale.

  3. 03

    Specification & Planning

    Dimensions, finishes and key installation details are agreed before fabrication begins.

  4. 04

    Making & Installation

    The piece is fabricated in the workshop and installed by the same team.

Why Artiling Studio

Surfaces planned as one calm composition.

A bespoke sink or tiled room should not look assembled from separate products. Basin, splashback, edge thickness, tap position, storage and veining are drawn together, so the grain runs through the mitre and the lines carry across the room.

  1. 01

    Designed from the beginning

    Finishes are chosen after seeing the room in its own light, rather than from a sample under showroom lighting.

  2. 02

    Joints planned around the material

    Mitres, tone matching and the transitions between surfaces are where the work shows. Each of those is resolved at drawing stage.

  3. 03

    From drawing to installation

    One studio can coordinate the bespoke porcelain sink, its fabrication and the surrounding surface work. Dimensions and joint lines then stay consistent across the project.

  4. 04

    Built for daily use

    A bathroom is used every day. Porcelain handles that, and the substrates and fixings behind it are specified to match.
